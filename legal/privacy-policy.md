## What we collect

| Data | Why | How long |
|------|-----|----------|
| Email address | Account identity | Until you delete your account |
| Tasks (todos, side quests) | Core app function | Until you delete them or your account |
| Session logs | Session history, opt-in analytics | Until you delete your account |
| Areas | Organising your tasks | Until you delete them or your account |
| Calendar event metadata (if you link a calendar) | Time tracking per area | Until you unlink the calendar |
| Accepted terms versions | Legal compliance | Until you delete your account |
| Settings | Your preferences | Until you delete your account |

We do **not** collect:
- Your location
- Your contacts
- Device identifiers for advertising
- Any data beyond what is listed above

---

## How we use it

- To run the app and sync your data across your devices
- To recover your data if you reinstall the app
- We do **not** sell, share, or use your data for advertising

---

## Where it's stored

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in the **EU region** (Frankfurt). Supabase is SOC 2 certified. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES).

---

## Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:
- **Access** a copy of your data — use "Download my data" in Settings
- **Delete** all your data — use "Delete my account" in Settings (irreversible)
- **Correct** your data — edit it directly in the app
- **Object** to processing — contact us and we'll respond within 30 days

---

## Calendar data

If you link a calendar, we read event titles and times to calculate how much time you've spent in each area. We do **not** write anything back to your calendar. You can unlink the calendar at any time from Settings.
